FROZEN DECISION POLICY

Targeted Operational Escalation Policy (τ = 0.9300)

Under capacity-constrained human review budgets (~25–30% of total volume), the operating threshold was mathematically frozen at τ = 0.9300 prior to test-set evaluation. This deliberately filters the high-confidence upper tail, ensuring that fewer than 6 out of 100 alerts are false alarms (>94% precision).
